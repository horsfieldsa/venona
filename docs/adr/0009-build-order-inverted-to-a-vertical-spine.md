# 2026-08-04 — Build order inverted to a vertical spine


The first plan was bottom-up: nine consecutive backend steps with nothing
visible until step 12 of 15, and the two hardest, most-deferrable subsystems
(acronym grounding, entity resolution) in the middle.

Two independent reviewers identified that as the most likely way the project
dies — two months in, no brief on screen, no evidence the product is worth
reading daily.

**Now:** S1 is one article through every layer to a working hover card. Seed
fixtures moved from step 10 to position 2, so the entire UI is developable with
zero model calls.
