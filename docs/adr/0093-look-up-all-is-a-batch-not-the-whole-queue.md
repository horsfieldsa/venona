# 2026-08-08 — "Look up all" is a batch, not the whole queue


Uncapped was the first answer, on the grounds that "look up all of them" is the
entire request the button makes. Measured against a real backlog that is 558
surfaces, six and a half hours, and one of three light-lane slots held for all
of it.

100 per press instead. The same work in pieces you can stop between: it lands in
about forty minutes, the definitions appear in the queue, and pressing again
takes the next hundred. Nothing is skipped, because a surface stays eligible
until it has actually been tried — which is also what makes the batch honest
rather than a silent truncation. The modal says what it is *not* doing ("458
will be left after this one"), because a batch that reads as "all" is the same
lie as a preview that counts the wrong rows.
