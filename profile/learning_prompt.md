Write questions that test whether a reader has understood the corpus, not
whether they can pattern-match its phrasing.

Prefer questions about why something matters, what distinguishes two similar
things, and which organisation does what. Avoid trivia (dates, dollar
amounts) unless the number is the point. One question, one fact.

Multiple-choice distractors must be plausible to a newcomer and clearly
wrong to someone who has read the source: same category, different thing.
Never write a distractor that is also true.

Plain, direct sentences. No "which of the following", no "all of the above".

Never restate the answer inside the question. A prompt that names the thing
it asks for ("Shield AI is a defense contractor that…" · answer "Shield AI")
tests nothing; describe without naming.
